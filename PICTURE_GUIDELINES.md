# Picture Selection Guidelines for IGCSE 4XES2 Task 3

## Image Requirements

### Technical Specifications
- **Format**: JPG or PNG (high quality)
- **Resolution**: Minimum 1920x1080 pixels (HD)
- **File Size**: Under 2MB for optimal loading
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 or 3:2 (landscape orientation)
- **Color Space**: sRGB for web compatibility

### Content Guidelines

#### 1. Homes and Living Spaces (homes.jpg)
**Required Elements:**
- People visible in or around homes
- Interior or exterior living spaces
- Diverse architectural styles
- People of different ages
- Clear focus on human interaction with space

**Ideal Composition:**
- Families or individuals in living rooms, kitchens, or outdoor spaces
- Multiple rooms or home environments visible
- People engaged in daily activities
- Good lighting showing textures and details

**Avoid:**
- Empty rooms without people
- Extreme close-ups (no context)
- Overly stylized or artistic interpretations
- Text-heavy images

#### 2. Travel and Tourism (tourism.jpg)
**Required Elements:**
- Tourists or travelers in destination settings
- People interacting with local culture or environment
- Clear sense of place and atmosphere
- Multiple people visible
- Activities typical of tourism

**Ideal Composition:**
- Tourists at landmarks, beaches, or cultural sites
- People taking photos, exploring, or experiencing local activities
- Mix of travelers and locals
- Dynamic scenes showing engagement
- Clear visual narrative

**Avoid:**
- Empty landscapes without people
- Extreme close-ups of single individuals
- Overcrowded scenes with no clear focus
- Commercial-style promotional shots

#### 3. Education and Learning (school.jpg)
**Required Elements:**
- Students in educational settings
- Teachers and classroom interaction
- Learning activities visible
- Diverse age groups appropriate for IGCSE level
- Classroom or educational environment

**Ideal Composition:**
- Students engaged in classroom activities
- Teachers instructing or facilitating
- Group work or individual study visible
- Modern or traditional classroom settings
- Educational materials and technology visible

**Avoid:**
- Empty classrooms
- Only teachers without students
- Extreme close-ups of materials
- Corporate training environments

#### 4. Working Life and Careers (work.jpg)
**Required Elements:**
- People in work environments
- Professional settings appropriate for young adults
- Diverse occupations and work situations
- Clear workplace context
- People engaged in work activities

**Ideal Composition:**
- Office workers, service industry, or skilled trades
- People collaborating or working independently
- Professional environments with visible tools/equipment
- Mix of ages and backgrounds
- Realistic workplace scenarios

**Avoid:**
- Extreme manual labor images
- Overly corporate stock photos
- People without work context
- Hazardous or unsafe work environments

## Selection Checklist

Before finalizing an image, ensure:

- [ ] Image shows people (primary focus)
- [ ] Resolution is at least 1920x1080
- [ ] File size is under 2MB
- [ ] People appear diverse and inclusive
- [ ] Scene is appropriate for IGCSE level students
- [ ] No offensive or controversial content
- [ ] Clear visual narrative or activity
- [ ] Good lighting and visibility
- [ ] Commercial use is permitted
- [ ] License allows redistribution (if applicable)

## File Naming Convention

Save images as:
- `homes.jpg` - Homes and Living Spaces
- `tourism.jpg` - Travel and Tourism
- `school.jpg` - Education and Learning
- `work.jpg` - Working Life and Careers

Place in: `C:\SpeakEd\static\images\pictures\`
